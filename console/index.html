06 / REFUNDS

ORIGINAL MINTER
CLAIM A PAID KEY

Each paid key can be refunded once after unsuccessful expiry. On v4, anyone may trigger the refund; payment goes to the original minter. Only that minter can choose an optional recipient. The key is burned and its original payment returned. Free treasury keys are ineligible.
