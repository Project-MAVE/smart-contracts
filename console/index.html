    03 / TREASURY

    TREASURY ROLE
    RESERVED ALLOCATION

    Free mint, plus gas. Allowed at any time before the deadline, also before Pioneer opens. Token IDs follow mint order: mint the whole allocation first to give the treasury the first IDs.

    SALE PROCEEDS
    —ETH

    Withdrawal unlocks when the complete collection has minted. The entire balance goes to the connected treasury wallet.

    KEPT-KEY PAYMENTS
    —ETH

    Without a sellout, the treasury can withdraw only what kept keys paid. Money for keys that can still be refunded stays in the contract.

    BURN TREASURY KEYS

    Without a sellout, free treasury keys are burned after the keep-or-refund window closes. Paid keys are refused.

    06 / REFUNDS

    ORIGINAL MINTER
    CLAIM A PAID KEY

    Each paid key can be refunded once after unsuccessful expiry. During the keep-or-refund window only the minter can refund their own keys; after it, anyone can, and payment goes to the original minter. Only that minter can choose an optional recipient. The key is burned and its original payment returned. Free treasury keys are ineligible.

    REFUND TELEMETRY
    —KEYS REFUNDED

    Refund eligibility follows the original minting wallet. NFT approvals do not transfer this right.

    Kept keys—
    Keep-or-refund window—
    KEEP OR REFUND

    After an unsuccessful expiry, each original minter has 96 hours to decide. Kept keys become transferable NFTs and their payment goes to the treasury; refunded keys are burned and their payment returns to you. Up to 50 keys in total, each in one list.

    !

    Keeping a key is final: a kept key can never be refunded.

    PENDING REFUND
    —ETH

    When a batch refund cannot reach your wallet, the amount waits here until you claim it.

    BATCH REFUND / ONE SIGNATURE

    Anyone can submit a batch. Each original minter receives the exact amount paid for their key. All IDs must be eligible and unique: any failure reverts the whole batch. During the keep-or-refund window only the minter's own keys are accepted, and a wallet that refuses ETH gets a pending refund instead of blocking the batch.
